The threat

Drone threats are evolving. Airspace protection must keep up.

Unauthorised drones can disrupt secure facilities, critical infrastructure, public events and industrial operations. Detection is often fragmented, and response can be slow.

Low-altitude threats

Small drones are hard to see, tell apart and follow across large sites.

Fragmented sensor awareness

Separate tools give separate pictures, not one clear view.

Delayed decisions

When alerts arrive late or unclear, operators lose time.

The solution

One integrated counter-drone workflow

STRIKE brings detection, tracking, threat prioritisation and response support into a single operational view.
