Product

What is STRIKE?

STRIKE (Smart Targeting, Reconnaissance and Intelligent Kill Engagement) is a family of AI-powered counter-UAS platforms from Anmay Defence. It detects, tracks and responds to hostile drones.

What is the difference between Gen 1 and Gen 2?

Gen 1 has one kinetic protection layer, AI vision, autonomous tracking and 360° coverage. Gen 2 adds a jamming layer, day and night optics, LiDAR-assisted identification and edge AI.

What are mission packages?

Modules you add to a platform: RF early warning, optical fiber link, electronic warfare, solar power and multi-weapon mounting. Gen 2 also offers radar surveillance, GPS spoofing and EMP payloads.

Is Gen 3 available?

No. Gen 3 is in research and development.

Is STRIKE made in India?

Yes. It is developed in India, which supports supply-chain security.

Technology

How far can STRIKE detect a drone?

Gen 1 detects a drone of 10 ft or more at up to 700 m. The RF early warning package senses drone radio signals from over 2 km. Gen 2 extends the optical range.

Which drones can it track?

Quadcopters, VTOL drones and fixed-wing drones.

Does it work at night?

Gen 2 has day and night PTZ cameras with 30× optical zoom.

How does it communicate?

Gen 1 uses LoRa (+1 km), WiFi and BLE. Gen 2 adds a 5 km optical fiber control link that resists jamming and a 2 km wireless video link.

Does an operator stay in control?

Yes. STRIKE works in auto mode or manual mode.

What power does it need?

24 VDC. The solar power package supports 24×7 operation in remote areas.
