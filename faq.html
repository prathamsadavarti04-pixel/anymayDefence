How do I request a briefing or pricing?

Contact us with your organisation, site type and timeline. Detailed datasheets, pricing and lead times are shared under controlled disclosure — not published on this site.

Can I see a live demonstration?

Trial footage is in our renamed archive (ASSET_MAP.md). Live/evaluated demonstrations are arranged for qualified defence and infrastructure customers.

What is STRIKE Gen1 vs Gen2?

Gen1: 1-layer kinetic, 700 m AI detection, 360°/75°, 5 trigger units, <10 min deployment. Gen2 adds day/night 30× PTZ, LiDAR, NVIDIA Edge AI, 360°/80° and a second EW layer, 2 km wireless / 5 km fiber.

Which payloads are supported?

Modular multi-weapon mounts (INSAS, AK-203, LMG, MMG & compatible) plus RF, OF, EW, SP, M, Radar and GNSS packages. EMP/swarm/directed-energy are Future/R&D.

Does it work at night and in jamming?

Gen2 is day/night with LiDAR; the optical-fiber package gives jammer-resistant C2/video over 5 km+. EW package adds multi-band jamming.

Auto or manual operation?

Both — autonomous search/detect/track/respond, or full manual operator control for complex environments.

What is the legal company name?

Anmay Defence is a brand of Anmay Autonomous Systems Pvt. Ltd., Mumbai, India — an initiative by Robonut Project.

Can I invest or partner?

Yes — technology partners, defence organisations, system integrators, R&D partners and strategic investors are invited to contact us.
